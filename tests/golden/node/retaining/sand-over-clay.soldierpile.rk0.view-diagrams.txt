DA1/2 (A2 + M2) — embedment, design excavation. Depth from the retained surface. Pressures are the unfactored ordinates per unit width of the acting face (retained side incl. surcharge; excavation side = passive resistance, per pile below the excavation for the Brinch Hansen model); the net pressure, V and M are factored with this branch's partial factors and multiplied by the acting widths (per pile). The V/M tail beyond the free-earth closure is set to zero (no toe reaction is modelled beyond it).
