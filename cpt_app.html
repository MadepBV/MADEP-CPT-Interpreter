Robertson (1990) — SBT / Ic

Normalised Qt and Fr. Stress-dependent — accounts for depth. Preferred for HS parameter derivation and PLAXIS modelling.

CUR 3 / NEN 6740

Direct qc and Rf ranges. Dutch–Flemish practice. No stress normalisation. Accepted for SB260 and building permit contexts.

NEN Tabel 3 / EC7

Karakteristieke grondparameters. Grond-, zand-, leem-, klei-, veensoorten op basis van qc-bereik en Rf. Geeft direct γ, φ'k, c'k en cu,k.

Tuning — m fitting ⚠ experimental

Experimental. The fitted m replaces the type-default only when the engineer explicitly accepts it per layer. Fit quality is shown as R² — values below 0.5 indicate too much scatter or insufficient depth range; keep the default in that case.
